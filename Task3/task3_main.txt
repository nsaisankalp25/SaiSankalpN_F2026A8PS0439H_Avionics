AEOLUS AVIONICS – TASK 3
PROPULSION SYSTEM

Selected Configuration:

    Frame       - 10-inch quadcopter
    Motors      - 4 × Holybro S500 V2 2216-920KV
    Propellers  - 4 × Pro-Range 1045 (10 × 4.5)
    ESCs        - 4 × 30 A
    Battery     - 4S 5000 mAh 30C LiPo





> WEIGHT CALCULATION

The estimated dry weight of the drone ( the frame, motors, propellers and ESCs ) is:
    ~950 g

Task 1 sensing system:  ~120 g

Task 2 communication system:  ~50 g

Battery:  ~500 g ( conservative and most common weight for a 4S battery )

Estimated total:

    950 + 120 + 50 + 500
    = 1620 g

If we consider other components, like wiring, mounts, connectors, the weight will roughly add upto 1700g

Design AUW is approximately:  ~1.7 kg

The task says the max weight is 2.5kg, so we are left with approx. 0.8kg as headroom and can be used for future upgrades.






> MOTOR AND PROPELLER SELECTION

The specified motor is the Holybro S500 V2 2216-920KV-CCW, paired
with 10 × 4.5 inch propellers.

The four motors act as 4 independent sources of Thrust, all controlled by their individual ESCs, being powered by one big 4S Battery.





> THRUST REQUIREMENT

For a design AUW of approximately 1.7 kg, the total thrust required just to hover is approximately: 1.7 * g = 16.6N

For each motor with the 10x4.5 prop:  1.7 / 4 * g = 4.16N     ( 425g thrust per motor )

Therefore, each motor needs approximately: 4.16N thrust for hovering.


According to the charts for Motor and Prop config:
30% throttle → ~215 g thrust → 23.5 W
50% throttle → ~470 g thrust → 61.5 W
100% throttle → ~1367 g thrust → 277 W

So the drone uses approximate 50% of throttle for stable hovering.

When we consider additional thrust requirements for phases like climbing, fast acceleration and movement, the motors would give out more power, consuming higher energy, hence, we will be choosing a propulsion system that has enough headroom for the above.






> ESC SELECTION

Four 30 A ESCs are used, one for each motor.

Total rated ESC current:  4 × 30A = 120A

The motor's maximum tested power is ~277 W at 4S ( per motor ); Current draw would be  ~18.7A per motor.
With a 1.5× safety margin: 18.7 × 1.5 = ~28 A, so a 30 A ESC per motor is selected for sufficient current headroom.






> BATTERY SELECTION

A 4S 5000 mAh LiPo battery is selected.

Nominal voltage: 14.8 V
Nominal battery energy: 14.8 V × 5 Ah = 74 Wh
Battery rating: 30C

Maximum theoretical continuous current: 5 Ah × 30C = 150 A

Sum of all ESC ratings: 4 × 30 A = 120 A
Average motor current: ~19 A per motor
Total average motor current: 19 × 4 ≈ 76 A

The 150 A battery rating provides sufficient current headroom above the estimated ~76 A motor current.
The 30 A ESC rating also provides current headroom for each individual motor during heavy load conditions such as acceleration and climbing.





> POWER and ENDURANCE

Estimated power consumption during the stable part of flight:

    Task 1 = ~10 W
    Task 2 = ~16 W
    Propulsion:
	Using each motor thrust 4.16N and comparing it with the official power charts of the motors, each motor will use approx. 58W.
	Total: 58W x 4 = 232W


Total: 
    10 + 16 + 232 = 258 W

Rounding to 260 W (Upper safer side)

Battery energy:  14.8V x 5Ah = 74Wh

Using 80% of the nominal battery capacity as usable energy (20% as reserve): 74 × 0.8 = 59.2 Wh

Estimated endurance: 59.2 / 260 × 60 = ~13.5 minutes
Roughly 13.5 min of flight time.

This is above the required 12 minute minimum endurance.
