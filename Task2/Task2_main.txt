AEOLUS AVIONICS – TASK 2
COMMUNICATION ARCHITECTURE

Selected Systems:
    RC + Telemetry - 2.4 GHz ExpressLRS
    Video          - DJI O3 Air Unit






> RC AND TELEMETRY

A 2.4 GHz ExpressLRS receiver is used for RC control.


A separate Telemetry radio is not required as the same ELRS supports for MAVLink Telemetry. Hence, we save on power and weight

The receiver is connected to a UART on the Pixhawk 6C Mini.

Receiver TX -> Pixhawk RX
Receiver RX -> Pixhawk TX
5 V -> 5 V
GND -> GND

The serial connection uses CRSF for normal ELRS operation.

For this mission, a target range of around 3–5 km LOS is considered sufficient. 
ELRS can provide much higher range in suitable conditions, so the selected range gives a plausible operating margin.







> VIDEO TRANSMISSION

The DJI O3 Air Unit is selected for live video transmission.

The O3 has a dedicated digital Video link with its own camera, hence, it doesn't depend on external video processing 
and transmission.


The O3 Air Unit is connected to the main battery through the power
system. Its input range is 7.4–26.4 V, so it is compatible with the
selected 4S LiPo battery.

The O3 uses a separate 5.8 GHz transmission band, keeping the video
link separate from the 2.4 GHz ELRS control-telemetry link.

The O3 has a manufacturer-rated maximum video transmission range of
up to 10 km under FCC conditions. For our design, the intended operating range is around 3–5 km.








> POWER

The communication system is powered from the main battery through
the drone's power distribution/power module.

No separate battery is planned for Task 2 in the initial design.

Estimated Task 2 power consumption during normal operation:

	ELRS: 0.5-0.75W
	O3 Camera module: ~15w
	

    	TOTAL ~16 W

The exact consumption will vary with transmission conditions and
operating mode, so 16 W is used as a representative value.






> WEIGHT

Estimated communication-system weight:


	O3 Unit along with wiring: ~40g
	ELRS receiver + antenna: ~5g
	Mounts and Wiring: ~5g

	TOTAL   ~50 g






> Clarifications over PPM, PWM, CRSF, etc

Digital Communication provides better reliability and allows multiple channel communication - RC, Telemetry, etc - over a single link.
Though analog systems are simpler, they are prone to interference and not very flexible.
Here we are using CRSF protocol with ELRS to establish a stable, fast and efficient connection.
One of the other reasons for not using Analog systems is due to mismatch of PPM/PWM; digital systems have almost no mismatches.






> FINAL COMMUNICATION ARCHITECTURE

RC Control: 2.4 GHz ExpressLRS

Telemetry:  MAVLink over the same ELRS link

Flight Controller Interface:  UART using TX/RX

Video:  DJI O3 Air Unit

Video Link:  5.8 GHz digital transmission

Power:   Main 4S LiPo through the power system

Estimated weight:  ~50 g

Estimated power:   ~16 W

Target operating range:  ~3–5 km LOS
