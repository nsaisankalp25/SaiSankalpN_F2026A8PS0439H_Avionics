AEOLUS AVIONICS – TASK 1 BONUS
CAMERA ORIENTATION and ALTERNATIVE BUILD


> CAMERA ORIENTATION

The OAK-D Lite will be mounted at the front of the drone, facing
forward along the direction of flight.

The camera will be kept pretty much horizontal with a small
downward tilt. This gives a useful view of both the area ahead and
the ground below the drone.

The Stereo Cameras should be strictly horizontal, away from any propellers, and if possible, far away from motors, for added 
stability.






>  ALTERNATIVE BUILD

OAK-D camera + NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.


The camera handles the main vision and AI processing, while the
Jetson handles higher-level navigation and detection tasks.

This build can be considered due to its high computational power and more headroom, main downside being its power consumption and cost.

This Build is best suited for Detection and Navigation Heavy workload more than a balanced flight.
